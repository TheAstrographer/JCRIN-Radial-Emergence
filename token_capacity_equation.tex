Quadratic → Linear Token Capacity Calculation  
Core Equations

1. Classical Quadratic Cost
\[
C_{\rm classical}(N) \propto N^{2}
\]

2. JCRIN Linear Cost
\[
C_{\rm JCRIN}(N) \propto N
\]

3. Equal-Budget Capacity Relation
Under a fixed compute / energy budget:
\[
C_{\rm classical}(N_{\rm classical}) = C_{\rm JCRIN}(N_{\rm JCRIN})
\]

\[
N_{\rm classical}^{2} \;\approx\; N_{\rm JCRIN}
\]

4. Leading-Order Solution
\[
N_{\rm JCRIN} \approx \bigl(N_{\rm classical}\bigr)^{2}
\]

5. Thinnest-Triangle Correction
\[
N_{\rm JCRIN}^{\rm corrected} \approx \bigl(N_{\rm classical}\bigr)^{2} \cdot \cos^{2}\Psi
\]

where
\[
\cos^{2}\Psi \approx 0.9775
\]

6. Explicit Example (\(N_{\rm classical} = 10^{5}\))
\[
\begin{align*}
N_{\rm JCRIN} &\approx (10^{5})^{2} = 10^{10} \\[6pt]
N_{\rm JCRIN}^{\rm corrected} &\approx 10^{10} \times 0.9775 = 9.775 \times 10^{9}
\end{align*}
\]

7. Capacity Multiplier
\[
\text{Multiplier} \approx \frac{N_{\rm JCRIN}}{N_{\rm classical}} \approx N_{\rm classical} \cdot \cos^{2}\Psi
\]

Compact Master Equation

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm JCRIN} \;\approx\; N_{\rm classical}^{2} \cdot \cos^{2}\Psi
}
\]
